Easy Grader

A private, single-family tool for keeping up with a student's schoolwork.

What it does

  • Summarizes a student's grades, missing work, and upcoming assignments in one place for their family.
  • Produces a short daily to-do sheet to help the student build good habits.
  • Emails scheduled reports to family members the account owner chooses.

How it uses Google

Easy Grader asks for one Google permission: send email on your behalf (gmail.send), plus your email address so it knows which account it is sending from. It uses this only to send the reports you set up. It cannot read, search, or delete your email.

Who it's for

Easy Grader is a personal tool run by its owner on their own computer. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.
