Privacy Policy

Easy Grader · Effective October 6, 2026

Easy Grader is a personal tool run by its owner on their own computer for their own family. It is not a public service.

Google user data we access

  • Send email on your behalf (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send) — used only to send the report emails the account owner schedules.
  • Your email address (openid, email) — used only to show which account is connected and as the sender address.

Easy Grader does not read, search, modify, or delete any email, contacts, or other Google data, and it requests no permission that would allow that.

How data is stored

The authorization token Google issues is stored only on the owner's computer, in a file readable only by the owner's account. Easy Grader has no servers, no database, no analytics, and no advertising.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. Report emails go only to recipients the account owner explicitly enters.

Limited Use

Easy Grader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can disconnect at any time from within Easy Grader, or by removing it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Easy Grader.
